# Privacy and support

## Privacy

The terms of service at <https://control.ampdat.com/terms> govern this section; if the two ever
differ, the terms win.

- **No account.** The service does not ask for your name or for an account.
- **What is logged for each request:**
  - the path and the response;
  - the time taken;
  - the user agent;
  - the paying or signed-in wallet address, and the settlement transaction, where there is one.
- **The hosting provider** also records the connecting IP address.
- **Use of the logs.** They are used to run, secure, price and improve the service, and are kept
  no longer than needed.
- **Wallets are public.** Wallet addresses and transactions are public on the blockchain anyway.
- **Analytics.** The web pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.
- **No sale.** Personal data is not sold.

The MCP tools only read public chain data. They do not see your conversation, only the
arguments the assistant sends: an address, a search query, or filters.

## Support

For questions, privacy requests, refunds and notices, email legal@ampdat.com.

To report a wrong answer, send:

- the address;
- the `as_of` block of the answer;
- what you expected.
